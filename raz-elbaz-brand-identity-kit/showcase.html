Accent rule and forbidden pairs

Color only where it means something. Neon marks the result, the selected row, focus and the one value that matters; warm hues mark the source; sun is the middle stage. A diagram line is one quiet color and changes only for a reason (the segment that reaches the result). The flow gradient lives in bands, washes and the progress ring, never on every line or bar. Brand hues never mean a state; states never decorate.

  • soft tier (any hue) on bg, surface, any light canvas: 1.46-1.98:1; the soft tier is for fills, tints and glow, never text or thin lines in light
  • line tier (any hue) on soft #E7F2F4, every wash end, neutral tint #ECE8E3: 2.93-2.95:1; charts sit on bg or surface; on a wash, strokes use the text tier (4.06 or more)
  • text tier (any hue) on wash ends #F8E0D5 #F8E1D1 #F2E6D3 #DCEAE8, result header #CDEAEF: 4.08-4.21:1; text on washes is ink or muted, the result header carries ink only
  • neon text #20778A on selected tint #E3F2F3: 4.49:1; text on a selected row is always ink
  • muted #9AA4AE (dark) on result header #2C515C (dark): 3.40:1; the result header carries ink only
  • muted #5F6670 / #9AA4AE (NULL label) on hatch line #E1E0DF / #394047: 4.40 / 4.15:1; the hatch stops under the label (knock-out)
  • quiet bar #DDDCDB / #3D444B on bg #F7F2EC / #11161B: 1.23 / 1.84:1; every quiet bar gets a 1.5px muted top line (5.21 / 7.19) or a printed value
  • light line or text tier on any dark canvas: in dark every tier is the soft tier
  • light logo blue #2999B1 on soft #E7F2F4 or a wash: below 3:1 (2.93); the light logo sits on bg, surface or white
  • rule #DDD7CF, grid #EDE9E4, hatch #E1E0DF, fill #DBD7D4 (and dark) on bg, surface: 1.02-1.57:1 by design; decorative structure, never the only carrier of meaning

Ink on bg 9.97 (dark 15.05) · muted on bg 5.21 (7.19) · neon text 4.64 on bg, 5.00 on surface · line tiers 3.00-3.26 · state text on its tint 4.64-5.35 (dark 5.66-7.64). Every text pair in use passes AA.

Motifs (8)

motifs/*.svg

Max 2 decorative motifs per composition, in zones, never scattered; few big shapes. A motif used as content (the explaining diagram, NULL cells and check marks in a table, the legend that keys a chart) follows the data and does not count as decoration. The schema map counts as one motif. Inline an SVG to get theme colors; as an img it shows the light fallbacks.

1. quiet-grid
full-bleed · large · tiled

A square grid at 5% ink (#EDE9E4), no numbers, no coordinates; always lighter than the table rule #DDD7CF; gives order without being seen.

2. data-line
between-sections · medium · once

One quiet line (muted, 1.5) with right-angle turns; only the segment that reaches the result turns neon line tier at 2.5, and its end point glows.

3. annotation-leader
edge-rail · small · 2-3

A thin elbow line (muted, 1) from a clause to a small square end in the sky line tier; a margin note for one part of a query.

4. null-hatch
badge · small · 2-3

A soft 45 degree hatch at 14% ink (#E1E0DF) in a cell with no value, always with the word NULL; the hatch stops under the word (knock-out).

5. check-marks
badge · small · 2-3

Correct, note, wrong marks on the checked row itself, each row on its state tint, marks drawn as geometry; the wrong row is struck through in muted.

6. legend-strip
between-sections · small · once

Named color squares under a hairline rule at the foot of an output; every square carries its name. Keying a compare chart, the squares use the line tier.

7. progress-ring
badge · medium · once

How much of a chapter is done: a ring, the arc solid light neon #5CC3D9, no gradient, with the neon ring glow, the rest in fill gray, the percentage always printed inside in ink.

8. schema-map
full-bleed · large · once

The big visual: tables as regions with soft header bands (.45, result .5; dark .35), relations as quiet muted routes, a join and a selection node, the result table outlined in neon with a glowing entry point.

Charts: three modes, chosen by the question

Charts sit on bg or surface, never on a wash. Quiet bars carry a muted top line; sequence bars a 1px neon outline; compare legends use the line tier.

Composition and rules

learning first

Learning first

A diagram must read first. Lines are one quiet color; color changes only with a real reason (the segment that reaches the result gets the accent and the glowing end point). The concept is never forced.

Glow on data only

Around a data point, an active line or progress. Never behind text; off in print; stronger in dark.

The gradient means something

Warm is the source, cool is the result. It lives in bands, backgrounds and the ring, not in every line and bar.

States are reserved

Green correct, amber note, red wrong or forbidden, blue selected. Never decoration; brand hues never mean a state.

One-hue background

The default background is one hue fading to off-white. Sunrise and sea together only when the screen has a source and a result.

Chart by question

Highlight when one or a few things matter; compare when all categories matter; sequence when values run small to large.

Quiet grid

5% only, always lighter than the table rule. No numbers, no coordinates.

Soft NULL

Hatch at 14% ink. The cell reads empty without shouting; the hatch stops under the word.

Bidi

Hebrew sentences avoid a number, a colon and inline code side by side.
