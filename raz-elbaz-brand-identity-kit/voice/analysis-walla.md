# Walla columns: craft analysis

Corpus: 30 columns and reviews from Walla, 12 writers, 2008 to 2017, about 23,000 words (list in `raz-elbaz-brand-identity-kit/voice/sources.md`, raw text in `raz-elbaz-brand-identity-kit/voice/corpus/`, not in git). Culture critics dominate (17 pieces); the rest are parenting (4), a humor column about a technophobe learning games (4), sports (4) and food (1). No political op-eds.

This file is about **craft only**: how sentences, paragraphs, openings, turns and endings are built. The topics and opinions of the articles are deliberately left out. Short quotes (a few words) are there only to point at a technique; nothing here is meant to be reused as text.

## Summary: what the good ones do

### Openings
- **Concrete first.** About half open on a thing you can see: a number (05), a two-word quote (20), a scene (03, 06, 21), a single image (07). Nobody opens by announcing the topic or defining a term.
- **Pattern, then break.** About a fifth open with a repeated frame and a turn: "אמרו ש..." x3 (01), "לא ברור..." x3 then "אבל מה שברור" (25), five one-word sentences (14).
- **Never** "בכתבה הזו נסקור", never a definition as a first line.

### Rhythm
- The median sentence is 12 words, but the spread is the point: 26% of sentences are 6 words or fewer and 17% are 25 or more. Long sentences build; a short one lands.
- **Fragments as verdicts.** Verbless or near-verbless lines close a paragraph: "למי אכפת.", "פיהוק.", "הוא הבוס.", "זה לא קרה.", "סיפור סגור."
- **Triads, often with the third item breaking the pattern**: "יותר מדי בכירים, יותר מדי מקורבים, יותר מדי שכונה." A list can hide the punchline in its last slot (05).
- **Repetition for effect**, used once per piece at most: "ושוב. ושוב. ושוב.", "ל-ב-ד."

### Turns and transitions
- **"אבל" is the hinge.** It is by far the most frequent connector (6.9 per 1,000 words, more than double Raz's chat and the site). A paragraph builds one way and "אבל" turns it.
- **Answer the reader's question before they ask it**: "למה? כי...", "האם זו אשמתו? ברור שלא.", "מה זה X, אתם שואלים?", "למה לייזרים, אתם שואלים? ובכן,".
- Spoken connectors carry the flow: אז, ואז, בקיצור, כך או כך, בכל מקרה, ובכן, "בואו נדבר על", "קחו, למשל,".
- **Few colons** (4.2 per 1,000 words). When a colon appears it is a signpost label ("הבעיה:", "הרקע פשוט:") or introduces a quote, not a slogan structure.

### Humor and irony
- **The narrator takes the joke.** The funniest pieces (the technophobe, the dad) make the writer the beginner, the klutz, the one who doesn't get it. The reader is never the target.
- **Self-correction as a joke**: "טוב, אז אולי את אחמד המצאתי. אז מה.", "תיקון — ...".
- **Parenthetical winks**, one or two per piece: "(ממש כך)", "(לא? זה רק אני?)".
- **Local, physical comparisons** instead of abstractions: a straw boat in a hurricane, a nuclear shelter's absorbency.
- **Twisted idioms**: a familiar phrase bent slightly ("לא בשר ולא חלב, סתם פרווה"; "לא ממציא את הגלגל... רק גורם לו לנסוע טוב יותר").
- **Jargon handled out loud** (08, 09, 10): the beginner narrator meets a term, admits it sounds absurd, and translates it in the same breath ("PP... Party, please"). For a site that teaches SQL to beginners, this is the most directly useful technique in the corpus.

### Endings
- **Callback** is the most common ending (about 12 of 30): the last line returns to the headline, the first image or a line from the middle (04, 06, 20, 23, 29).
- **A short final line** (about 8 of 30): "היא יודעת.", "יש לה, בעיקר, בעיה.", "עכשיו שישמרו עליו."
- **Almost never a summary.** Only the most formal writer uses "בסיכומו של דבר" or "אם כך".

## What does not transfer to RowdyQL

- **Length.** The median column paragraph is about 60 words. Site copy is short, and Raz asks for less text, not more.
- **Snark at people.** The critics mock celebrities. The site has no one to mock except itself.
- **Formal literary register.** One writer (שביט) uses "חפץ", "עמה", "לבטח", "אינו". That is the "אקדמית מסורבלת" register Raz rejects.
- **Stacked wordplay and pop-culture references.** One device per piece works; several at once turn into "מסוגנן" in the bad sense Raz named.
- **Long comma chains.** The columns average 93 commas per 1,000 words. The existing copy rules ask for one idea per sentence.
