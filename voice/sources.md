**How I chose.** All 30 are from Walla, found through Walla's own monthly archive pages (`/archive/<category>?year=&month=`), which list the writer for each item. I screened candidates by their opening, their close and the clean word count, and kept writers with a recognizable voice: short sentences mixed with long ones, irony, and a callback or twist at the end. I left out news, wire copy, sponsored pieces, listicles, roundups, interviews and guest corporate op-eds. Nothing is a political or security op-ed. Two music columns (15, 22) touch on politics or national symbols in passing.

**Spread.** There are 12 writers. עינב שיף, ארז מיכאלי and רותם ברוכין have 4 each, the cap. The heaviest group is culture critics: TV, music and film make up 17 of the 30. The rest are 4 sports, 4 parenting ("חיים של אבא" in וואלה בריאות), 4 tech/gaming and 1 food. Dates run from 2008-02 to 2017-03, all before 2022: 7 from 2008–2009, 7 from 2010–2011, 11 from 2013–2014, 4 from 2015 and 1 from 2017.

**Gaps and caveats.** Food is thin, with 1 piece. The good 2009 café column (תיגל נשיא, syndicated from mouse.co.il) runs under 500 words, and the 2012–2018 restaurant reviews read flatter. Travel and cars had no piece that met the bar.

**Extraction (updated).** The first download pulled CSS and photo captions into the text, because the script's `<p` pattern also matched `<picture>` and `<path>` tags. `fetch_article.py` was fixed to keep only Walla's body paragraphs (`article_speakable`), and all 30 files were downloaded again under the same names. The word counts in the files are now clean, from 507 to 1,234. A few pieces still carry one to five "related article" headlines or a stray lyric line: 07 and 25 at the end, 24 and 30 in the middle. They are easy to spot and were ignored in the analysis.
