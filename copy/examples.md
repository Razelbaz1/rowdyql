# Raz's before/after pairs

The highest authority on tone. Raz's own rewrites of strings that sounded wrong; one row each.

| before | after (Raz) | why |
|---|---|---|

The colon became a dash, the future tense became present, and it ends on a short turn. The real term stays. Approved 2026-09-30 (voice profile v1). |
